Why Meadow & Paws

Little comforts. Big tail wags.

From the first morning walk to the last curl-up of the day, discover essentials that fit naturally into life with your pet.

Play with purpose. Rope toys, fetch favorites, and curious-cat playthings bring variety to supervised playtime.

A softer place to rest. Cozy beds and blankets create a familiar corner for naps after a busy day.

Ready for a walk. Explore collars, leashes, and harnesses, choosing the correct size and fit for your companion.

Daily care, made simple. Feeding bowls, grooming basics, and practical accessories help keep everyday routines organized.

Our Pet Shop

A little neighborhood love for every pet.

Meadow & Paws is a pet shop concept inspired by Madison, Wisconsin: friendly neighborhoods, lakeside walks, and homes made happier by four-legged company. Our collection brings together useful, inviting essentials for dogs and cats.

Picture a soft bed beside your favorite chair, a basket of well-loved toys, and a leash ready by the door. Different essentials, one idea: making the time you spend with your pet a little more joyful.
